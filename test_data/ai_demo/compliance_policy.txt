EXPENSE & VENDOR PAYMENT COMPLIANCE POLICY
Effective: 2026-01-01

1. APPROVAL THRESHOLD
   All vendor payments and expense transactions exceeding $1,000 must be
   reviewed and approved by an authorized manager before processing.
   Transactions above this threshold with no approver on file are a
   policy exception.

2. CATEGORY CLASSIFICATION
   Every transaction must be assigned to one of the approved expense
   categories (Office Supplies, Travel, Software, Consulting, Utilities,
   Marketing). Transactions with no category assigned cannot be properly
   allocated to a budget line and should be flagged for correction.

3. DUPLICATE PAYMENT PREVENTION
   No transaction should be recorded more than once. Any exact-duplicate
   transaction record indicates a possible double payment and must be
   investigated before the vendor is paid a second time.

4. LARGE PAYMENT REVIEW
   Any single transaction exceeding $10,000 is considered a large payment
   and requires senior management review regardless of category, in
   addition to standard approval.

5. VENDOR COMMUNICATION QUALITY
   Recurring issues with vendor responsiveness or invoice accuracy (e.g.
   late invoices, unresolved billing errors) should be tracked and
   considered during annual vendor review.
